Symbiote

A companion that remembers the favours between you — and answers by citing the entry and its date.

Open Symbiote

“Settled on 22/11/2026: lent me his drill (owed by me, Ravi). — from 1 ledger entry.”

Status Built and working: favours recorded in both directions with a date; settling moves an entry to settled with the date it settled; an answer that is assembled from entries and cites each one's date; totals that add up; and a refusal to invent a favour when the ledger has nothing.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: a language model. There is none, and the answers are assembled from the ledger with fixed phrasings — which is why the app can always show its work.

What it does

  • Records a favour — owed by you or owed to you — with a date.
  • Settles an entry, and keeps it: a settled entry is not deleted, because the citation has to survive.
  • Answers a question by citing the entries it used, with their dates.
  • Refuses to answer about someone the ledger does not know, rather than inventing a favour.

What it will not do

  • Invent a favour. With nothing to cite, it says so.
  • Answer without a citation. Every line names its entry's date.
  • Use a model. No endpoint, no key, no completion.
  • Delete a settled entry — the ledger is the memory.
  • Use the internet. No fetch, no endpoint; CSP connect-src 'none'.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputNo person, no words, a nonsense direction, an empty question, settling an entry that is not there or is already settled — each refused with the field named.
Nothing to citeThe answer says the ledger has nothing, and that it will not make one up.
No networkNothing changes; the ledger is on the device.
Storage full or unavailableNOT SAVED, and the ledger on screen is unchanged.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export; never replaced with an empty one — that looks like nobody owes anybody anything.

How to run it

  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js.
