What it does

Records a favour in either direction with a date, settles it without deleting it, and answers questions by assembling the sentence from entries — with the date attached to every line.

What it refuses

Ask about someone the ledger does not know and it says so: “I have nothing about Meena in the ledger. I will not make one up.”
